# must_stay_live

Programs with a real discrepancy that NO dismissal may touch (`typediff calibrate` reports a dismissed one as a
violation, see `typediff/calibration.py`). The directory is empty on purpose: the two programs it held on Days 2 to 6
(`ty_constant_fold_tuple_subclass.py`, `mypy_stale_alias.py`) are known duplicates of open ty issues #2171 and #2792, and
the Day-7 decision was to close those shapes with narrow entries. They moved to `../known_duplicates/`.
